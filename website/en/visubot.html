Free · no sign-up · with or without a robot

Program the mBot2. With structograms and Java.

With VisuBot you build programs for the mBot2 learning robot as a structogram or write them in Java. The 3D simulator shows right away where it drives. Connect the real mBot2 with a USB cable and it drives along.

Start VisuBot in the browser Begin with lesson 1

The simulator runs in any browser. For the real mBot2 you need Chrome or Edge and a USB cable.

visustruct.org/app/?visubot

How it works

From structogram to moving robot in three steps

  1. 1

    Build

    Drag mBot2 commands like “move forward” and “turn right” into the structogram, or type them in Java.

  2. 2

    Test

    The 3D simulator shows right away which path your program drives. No robot needed.

  3. 3

    Drive

    Connect the real mBot2 with the USB cable. It drives your program step by step.

Features

Everything your mBot2 can do

Motors, sensors, light and sound: every part of the robot has its own commands.

Drive and turn

Forward, backward, turning or each wheel on its own, at your own speed.

Read the sensors

Measure distance, line, colours, light, loudness and tilt, and react to them.

Light, sound and screen

Light up the five LEDs, play melodies and show text or emotions on the screen.

mBot2 live

After every change VisuBot runs your program and shows the drive in the 3D simulator.

Structogram and Java live

Type Java code and the structogram builds itself alongside, and the other way round.

Real robot, nothing to install

The mBot2 connects over a USB cable straight to Chrome or Edge. No program, no account.

Learning area

Learn robotics, step by step

22 lessons lead from the first square to your own methods. You try each lesson right next to it, in the simulator or with the real mBot2.

  • Lesson 1: a 3D tour of every part of the mBot2
  • Exercises that VisuBot checks automatically in the simulator
  • Projects such as a line follower, an alarm system and parking
  • Reference: every mBot2 command with examples

Without a robot

VisuStruct: structograms for any program

VisuBot is part of VisuStruct. With VisuStruct you build structograms without a robot, test them step by step and turn them into Java, Python or JavaScript. You can switch at any time in the gear menu.

  • Dedicated lessons and tasks on structograms
  • Simulation with variables and output
  • Also as an app for Mac, iPad, iPhone and as a Java app

Privacy policy – use of VisuStruct

Privacy-friendly (GDPR): VisuStruct does not collect or store personal data. Your structograms and .visustruct files stay under your control — you decide whether to keep them on your device or back them up (for example) in your own cloud.

If you use linked services related to the app (e.g. App Store, GitHub), the privacy terms of those providers apply; they may process data there. This website is served from a server; as usual, technical server log data is generated.
